It is Almost Morning
Completed in 2025.

The video follows a patch of sunlight traversing an empty home. The walls are painted white so the changing light is the only source of color, and the audio is visual-reactive so the light is the only source of sound.

I plan to continue this project once I have access to a video camera again, and will seek out new architecture for the light to traverse. Continuing this project means asks the question "what is architecture for sunbeams?"


Process
First, banks of film were shot with the processing that would later be done to them in mind. Then the videos were spliced together, and taken into TouchDesigner. There, a script calculates how present colors cyan, magenta, yellow, black, and white are in each frame. This "presence" value for each color maps to the gain and left-right pan of a corresponding audio track. The tracks are synthetic instruments created in Ableton Live, which is communicating with TouchDesigner in real time via TDAbleton. The pan and gain were recorded to the tracks, then manually recomposed and polished for the final result.

This patch leaves room for live manipulation and performance. It was installed as a single channel looping video projection for a group show at Artists Image Resource in July '25.
